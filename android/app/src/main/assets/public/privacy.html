VOW Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 September 2026

1. Information we collect

VOW may collect account information, goals, plans, milestones, sessions, reviews, journal content, preferences, usage events, subscription status and information required for integrations you choose to connect.

2. How we use information

We use information to authenticate you, generate and execute plans, provide AI-assisted features, schedule notifications and calendar events, provide Premium features, prevent abuse, diagnose failures, maintain security and respond to support or data requests.

3. AI processing

Goal and coaching inputs may be processed by VOW infrastructure and third-party AI providers to provide requested features. VOW is designed to avoid sending unnecessary personal information. Do not submit information you do not want processed by the Service or its providers.

4. Connected services

When you connect services such as Google Calendar or Strava, VOW processes the information and permissions needed for the requested feature. OAuth credentials are stored server-side and are not included in account exports. Account deletion attempts to revoke supported external access.

5. Payments

Payment details are handled by the applicable payment provider. VOW may receive subscription identifiers, payment status and transaction metadata needed to provide Premium and prevent fraud.

6. Sharing

We may use service providers for cloud infrastructure, authentication, AI, calendars, payments, analytics and app distribution where reasonably necessary to operate the Service. We do not sell personal information.

7. Retention and deletion

We retain information while needed to operate the Service or meet legal, security or accounting obligations. VOW provides account export and account deletion controls where available.

8. Security

VOW uses authentication controls, database access policies, server-side entitlement checks, input limits, AI usage controls and other safeguards. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

9. Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or otherwise control personal information. Contact us for privacy requests.
